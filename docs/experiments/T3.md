# T3 · Shared-strip pair test

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T3. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §5.2. Diagnostic only: nothing is fitted on batch labels and the frozen model is not touched. Data: the 31 known images (`out/features.csv` built from `data/Batch_{1,2,3}` only).

## Pre-registered (committed before any distance was computed)

**Question.** Inside one physical strip, do a Batch_1 image and a Batch_2 image differ more than two images of the same batch from one strip?

**Pairs.** Every pair of images with the same `strip_group`. Layout checked against the review before running:

| Pair kind | n | Strips |
|---|---|---|
| Batch_1–Batch_2 | 3 | 2080, 2148, 2156 |
| Batch_2–Batch_3 | 5 | 2068 (3), 2080, 2272 |
| Batch_1–Batch_3 | 1 | 2080 |
| Same batch | 18 | Batch_3: 1612 (1), 1904 (3), 2060 (6), 2068 (3), 2088 (3); Batch_1: 2316 (1); Batch_2: 2048 (1) |

**Representations (fixed now; label-free).**

- **deep:** the `deep_` columns of the 31 images, standardised over the 31, reduced to 10 principal components fitted on all 31 (`qc.attribute.Reducer`), each component then z-scored over the 31.
- **material:** `usable_features(df, MATERIAL_FAMILIES)` (`reg, edge, tex, par, kpi`), z-scored over the 31 (`qc.attribute.Standardiser`, NaN → 0).

Distance: Euclidean in each representation.

**Tests.** Large distance = different.

1. **Pair level:** the rank-sum of the 3 Batch_1–Batch_2 distances among the 21 distances (3 + 18 same-batch). One-sided exact p over all C(21,3) = 1,330 subsets (share with rank-sum ≥ observed). Also reported: whether all 3 exceed all 18.
2. **Strip level (the one to quote):** one value per strip. Each of the 7 strips with a same-batch pair gives the mean of its same-batch distances; each of the 3 shared strips gives its Batch_1–Batch_2 distance. Exact rank-sum over C(10,3) = 120 subsets.
3. **Positive control:** the same two tests for the 5 Batch_2–Batch_3 pairs against the same 18. At strip level, the 3 strips (2068, 2080, 2272) are each represented by their mean Batch_2–Batch_3 distance, against the same 7: C(10,3) = 120.

**Reading rules.**

- If the 3 cross pairs sit inside the same-batch range (strip-level p ≥ 0.05), write: "inside a strip, the folder label is not visible; Batch_1 vs Batch_2 is a lean". Then stop the Batch_1/Batch_2 search.
- If the positive control also fails (strip-level p ≥ 0.05), the test is too blunt and the "no" is weak. Say so.
- Only if test 2 gives p < 0.05 for a representation: per material feature, take the 3 signed Batch_1 − Batch_2 differences divided by the SD of that feature's 18 same-batch pair differences. List features with the same sign in all 3 and each beyond 2 SD, at most 3, largest minimum |d| first. Observations only; nothing is fitted.

**Caveats, stated with the result.**

- 16 of the 18 same-batch pairs are Batch_3, and pairs share images (strip 2060 gives 6 pairs from 4 images), so the pair-level p is indicative.
- Distance along a strip is unknown except for 2080, where the images join edge to edge, so for that strip the test is conservative.
- The best possible strip-level p is 1/120 ≈ 0.008.

## Results (3 Oct 2026, run after the pre-registration commit `13217ad`)

Command: `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/experiments/T3_pair_test.py`. Outputs: `out/experiments/T3/result.json`, `pairs_deep.csv` and `pairs_material.csv`. Pair counts matched the pre-registered layout exactly. Material representation: 180 usable features.

**Exact one-sided rank-sum p** (small = cross-batch pairs differ more than same-batch pairs):

| Test | deep (10 PCs) | material (180) |
|---|---|---|
| Batch_1–Batch_2, pair level (3 vs 18, 1,330 subsets) | 0.59 | 0.91 |
| Batch_1–Batch_2, **strip level** (3 vs 7, 120 subsets) | **0.58** | **0.91** |
| Positive control Batch_2–Batch_3, pair level (5 vs 18) | 0.91 | 0.97 |
| Positive control Batch_2–Batch_3, **strip level** (3 vs 7) | **0.91** | **0.91** |
| All Batch_1–Batch_2 distances above every same-batch distance? | no | no |

**Distances, deep:**

- Batch_1–Batch_2: 3.28 (2080), 2.69 (2148), 2.25 (2156).
- Same-batch, per strip: 2.19 (2060) to 5.34 (1612); 6 of the 7 strips lie between 2.19 and 4.06.
- Batch_2–Batch_3: 1.27 (2080), 1.79–2.93 (2068), 2.38 (2272).

**Distances, material:**

- Batch_1–Batch_2: 13.4 (2080), 9.5 (2148), 7.9 (2156).
- Same-batch, per strip: 10.9–15.6.

**Readings, applied as pre-registered:**

1. Strip-level p ≥ 0.05 in both representations, so: **inside a strip, the folder label is not visible; Batch_1 vs Batch_2 is a lean.** The Batch_1/Batch_2 search stops here.
2. The positive control also fails (strip-level p = 0.91 in both). **The test is too blunt, and the "no" is weak.** Whole-image distances over all components or features do not pick out a Batch_2–Batch_3 difference inside a strip, although "Batch_3 or not" is detectable across strips (LOSO 0.85–0.88, PLAN_v4 §12).
3. The feature step (step 5) was not run: its condition (strip-level p < 0.05) did not hold.

**What this adds** (interpretation, not pre-registered):

- In both representations, cross-batch pairs inside a strip are, if anything, *closer* than same-batch pairs. Strip 2080 is the clearest case: its three images join edge to edge, and its Batch_2–Batch_3 distance (1.27) is the smallest of all 27 pairs.
- This fits the review's reading (§4, §8.3): what separates the folders lives mostly *between* strips, not between neighbouring images of one strip.
- For the pitch: "Batch_3 or not" generalises to held-out strips, but no measurement here shows two neighbouring images of one coating falling in different folders for a visible reason.
- Caveats as pre-registered: 16 of 18 same-batch pairs are Batch_3; pairs share images; positions along a strip are unknown except for 2080. Strip 2068 also contributes to both the reference and the positive-control side.
- The optional tile-level MMD (ticket step 6) was not run. It is reserved for an "unclear" result, and this one is a clear null.
