# T8 · Train-time augmentation (the original lighting idea, done properly)

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T8. Source: PLAN_v0's augmentation idea; MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §5.1 note, [L5]. Stacked on T1 (it reuses T1's perturbation functions and feature cache). Every model here is a v2 candidate only: the frozen model stays frozen (Pat, 3 Oct). Data: the 31 known images and their perturbed copies.

## Pre-registered (committed before any augmented model was fitted)

**Why.** In T1, σ = 5 noise flips "Batch_3 or not" for 12 of 17 Batch_3 images, and added dark-graphite zones move all 7 Batch_1 images to Batch_3. If the model sees such copies with unchanged labels, it should learn to ignore those directions. T9 suggests that, once acquisition is ignored, little signal may be left.

**Copies.** Feature rows of perturbed images, from T1's functions and seeds (`rng_for(image_id, perturbation)`), cached in `out/experiments/T1/perturbed_features.parquet`. Missing rows are computed first, so that **every image gets the same copies whatever its batch**. Augmenting one class only would itself correlate with the label.

- **Nuisance set:** `noise5, blur1, gain120, gamma125, shade125, curtain, hflip`, for all 31 images. To add: `noise5` and `blur1` for the 14 Batch_1/Batch_2 images.
- **Dark-graphite set:** `dg_add` for every image with dark share ≤ 0.1, and `dg_remove` for every image above 0.1, so each image gets exactly one. To add: `dg_add` for the 10 Batch_2/Batch_3 images at ≤ 0.1.

**Training, inside each LOSO fold** (no copy of a held-out image is ever used for fitting):

- Deep PCA (`Reducer`) and standardisation are fitted on the **original** training images only.
- Logistic regression on originals plus copies. Every row (original or copy) has sample weight 1 / (1 + K), where K is the number of copies of that image, so each image counts once. `class_weight="balanced"` as today.
- `C` from the nested inner LOSO over the usual grid, with the same augmentation inside the inner folds. The staged model follows the frozen recipe: Batch_3-or-not on the material families, which-variation on `deep`.

**Arms** (3 arms × 2 models = 6 list entries):

| Arm | Copies |
|---|---|
| (a) nuisance | the 7 nuisance copies |
| (b) nuisance + dark graphite | (a) plus the one dark-graphite copy |
| (c) feature jitter | 7 copies per image of the standardised training features plus Gaussian noise. σ per family = the median over the 7 nuisance perturbations of T1's per-family drift (`family_drift.csv`, Batch_3-SD units); deep PCs use T1's "deep PCs" column. Seed `crc32("<image_id>:jitter<k>")` |

Models: flat `deep`, and staged `material > deep`.

**Metrics** (all leave-one-strip-out):

1. Balanced accuracy, and correct calls per batch.
2. **Invariance** (the main expected gain), with the held-out images' own copies predicted by the fold model:
   - `stage_baseline` flips for Batch_3 under `noise5` and `blur1` (T1 arm B: 12 and 3 of 17);
   - for Batch_1 under `dg_add`, flips of the predicted batch (T1 arm B: 7 of 7).
3. **Out-of-fold right per confidence tier** (high ≥ 0.75, medium ≥ 0.5, low), uncalibrated.
   - Deviation from the ticket: no `--dry-run` repeats. The out-of-fold LOSO probabilities give the same kind of record.
4. **Nulls:** 200 segment shuffles, seed 0, with the same shuffles for all 6 entries. Fixed `C` = 0.1 in the null, as in `permutation_null`. Copies inherit the shuffled label. Reported per entry, and as the **best-of-6 null** (per shuffle, the maximum over the 6 entries).

**Reading rules.**

- An entry is a **v2 candidate** only if its LOSO is above the best-of-6 null p95 **and** its noise5 `stage_baseline` flips for Batch_3 are ≤ 3 of 17 (staged entries; for flat `deep`, `predicted` flips).
- If arm (b) brings Batch_1 to ≤ 1 of 7 correct and `dg_add` flips to ≤ 2 of 7, write: "made invariant to the dark-graphite contrast, the model loses Batch_1: the Batch_1 call rested on that contrast".
- If no entry passes, write: "augmentation does not produce an acquisition-robust model that still sorts the batches". This agrees with T9, and the frozen model stays as is.
