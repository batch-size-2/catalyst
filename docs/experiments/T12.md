# T12 · Tile-type pooling: let a local feature count

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T12. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §7.2, [L18], [L19]. Stacked on T11 (it reuses T11's InLens tile embeddings). A v2 candidate study; the frozen model is not changed. Data: the 31 known images, about 1,500 InLens tiles.

## Pre-registered (committed before any run)

**Why.** Today each image's deep features are the mean and SD over its tiles. A kind of region present in a few tiles is averaged away, and T11's heatmaps show the differences are patchy.

**Representations.** All fitted inside each LOSO training fold, on the training images' tiles only, label-free:

| Entry | Per-image features | Fit inside the fold |
|---|---|---|
| (i) tile-type shares | share of the image's tiles in each of k = 8 types | k-means (k = 8, `n_init=10`, seed 0) on the L2-normalised tile embeddings |
| (ii) tile quantiles | p10 and p90 over the image's tiles of each of 10 tile-level PCs (20 features) | PCA (10 components) on the tile embeddings |

**Classifier:** standardised, class-balanced multinomial logistic regression, `C` from the nested inner LOSO over the usual grid (as `qc.attribute`). Three-way.

**Null:** 200 segment shuffles, seed 0, fixed `C` = 0.1, the same shuffles for both entries. Reported per entry and as the best-of-2 null.

**Deliverable either way.** A fit of k = 8 on all 31 images' tiles (label-free), with 6 example tiles per type: the 6 nearest the type centre. Saved as `docs/experiments/T12_tile_types.png`, plus each type's share per batch. These are the example-based reasons on PLAN_v4 §5's open list.

**Reading rule.**

- An entry is a v2 candidate only if its LOSO balanced accuracy is above the best-of-2 null p95 **and** above flat `deep`'s 0.64 (PLAN_v4 §12.1).
- T9's caveat applies to anything built on InLens tiles.

## Results (4 Oct 2026, after the pre-registration commit `63e3d62`)

Command: `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/experiments/T12_tile_types.py`. Output: `out/experiments/T12/result.json`. The gallery is `docs/experiments/T12_tile_types.png`: rows are types 0–7, each showing the 6 tiles nearest the type centre.

| Entry | Three-way LOSO | Own null p95 | Best-of-2 null p95 |
|---|---|---|---|
| (i) tile-type shares, k = 8 | 0.56 | 0.52 | 0.55 |
| (ii) tile quantiles, p10/p90 of 10 tile PCs | **0.65** | 0.53 | 0.55 |
| flat `deep`, mean/SD pooling (PLAN_v4 §12.1, for reference) | 0.64 | 0.54 | – |

**Reading, as pre-registered:** (ii) passes, but only just. It is above the best-of-2 null (0.55) and above flat `deep` (0.64) by 0.01, about one image. It is a v2 candidate on paper. Two caveats decide how much that is worth:

- Across T8, T10 and T12 there are now 9 such entries tried against the same 31 images. A best-of-9 null would be higher than 0.55 (the review's best-of-7 null for Batch_1 vs Batch_2 was 0.86). On its own it is not evidence of a better model.
- It runs on InLens tiles, so T9's finding applies: what separates the folders is predictable from acquisition.
