## Pre-registered (committed before anything was measured)

**1. Silicon particle classes by rule.** From `particles()`, every labelled particle, area-weighted. Classes are checked in this order:

| Class | Rule |
|---|---|
| `dim_ragged` | `contrast_ratio < 1.75` and `solidity < 0.85` (the strip 2316 kind, review §8.2 Q1) |
| `porous` | not `dim_ragged`, and (`void_frac > 0.1` or `texture` > T) |
| `dense` | everything else |

- T is the 90th percentile of `texture` over all Batch_3 particles, unweighted, fixed from the baseline alone.
- Per image: the area share of each class (class area / all particle area).

**2. Binder share and its through-thickness gradient.**

- `binder_area_frac` = `Phase.BINDER` pixels / non-ignored pixels.
- `binder_slope`: the valid rows are cut into 10 equal horizontal bands. Fit a line to the band binder fraction against normalised depth (0 = top, 1 = bottom) and divide its slope by the mean band fraction.
- Limit stated now: `BINDER` today means thin bright rims plus dim Si-like objects (PLAN_v4 §3.2). The nanoporous "lace" the review describes is partly labelled pore or graphite. This is a proxy for the lace, not a measurement of it.

**3. Long thin horizontal pores (cracks, layer separation).**

- At work resolution (every second pixel of the full-resolution mask, which is upsampled from it), take connected `Phase.PORE` regions (8-connected) in the valid rows.
- Keep regions with major axis > 5 µm, major/minor axis ratio > 5, and major axis within 20° of horizontal (|skimage orientation| ≥ 70°).
- Report `hpore_per_1e4um2` (count per 10⁴ µm² of valid area) and `hpore_area_frac` (their area / all pore area).

**Table.** Per batch: mean with a 90% t-interval over images, the strip-level mean (one value per strip) beside it, and per image in the CSV.

**Reading rules.**

- **Sanity:** the 2 images of strip 2316 should be the top 2 of 31 on `dim_ragged` share. If they are not, the rule does not capture the 2316 kind; say so, and keep it out of the batch statements.
- A batch "differs" on a descriptor only if its 90% interval excludes the Batch_3 mean **and** the strip-level means agree in direction. Otherwise report "no clear difference". No p-values and no verdict: this is description for the T5 statements.
