# T2 · Dark-graphite share in InLens, and how much the deep stage leans on it

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T2. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §8.2 Q2, §8.4 item 1, §8.5 step 2. Diagnostic only; the frozen model is not changed. Data: the 31 known images (`data/Batch_{1,2,3}`). The 3 unseen samples are measured in step 5 only, report-only, after steps 1–4 are committed.

## Pre-registered (committed before anything was measured)

**Hypothesis.** The review judges the dark InLens graphite to be an electrical imaging contrast, not material (about 75% confidence). Its quarter-scale throwaway script found deep PC1 correlates −0.75 with the dark share. If the deep stage's Batch_1/Batch_2 lean rests on that contrast, removing it from the deep components should take the deep model down to its null.

**1. Descriptor** (`qc.measure.dark_graphite_share(mask, channels)`; report-only, not in `imaging()` or anything `qc/decide.py` reads):

- Graphite = `segment()` mask == `Phase.GRAPHITE`, valid rows only, InLens pixels at 255 excluded.
- InLens is black-subtracted (`black_level`), clipped at 0 and Gaussian-smoothed with σ = 1 px at full resolution. A graphite pixel is dark if its smoothed value is < 55.
- `dark_graphite_share` = dark / graphite over the valid rows; `dark_graphite_share_top` = the same over the top third of the valid rows.

**2. Table.** Per image, per batch (min, median, max) and per strip. Compared with review §8.2: Batch_1 0.01–0.05; Batch_2 0.00–0.28; Batch_3 0.00–0.80, six above 0.6. "Disagrees materially" = any batch range end off by more than 0.1, or the Batch_1 maximum ≥ 0.1.

**3. Correlations.** Transform the 31 images with the **frozen** model's variation-stage reducer (`model_parts(load_model())["variation"]["reducer"]`) into its 10 deep PCs. Report Pearson and Spearman r of each PC with both shares and with each `img_` descriptor in `out/features.csv`.

**4. Diagnostic LOSO** (not a new model; `qc.attribute.loso_cv` with nested `C`, unchanged otherwise):

- Optional `residualize` argument (default `None` = today's numbers exactly): inside each training fold, regress each deep PC on an intercept plus `dark_graphite_share` (OLS on the training rows). Classify on the residuals, applying the training-fold coefficients to the held-out rows.
- Four runs: flat `deep` and staged `material > deep`, each without and with residualisation.
- Null for each residualised run: `permutation_null`, 200 segment shuffles through the same residualised pipeline, seed 0.

**Reading rules.**

- If the residualised flat `deep` LOSO is at or below its null p95, write: "the Batch_1/Batch_2 lean rests on an imaging contrast and is expected to hold only for images from the same sessions". The same reading is reported separately for the staged model.
- PCs with |r| ≥ 0.5 (Pearson) against either share are listed for T4.
- The model stays frozen whatever the result.

**5. Unseen samples (report-only, after 1–4 are committed).** Both shares for `3e122cbj`, `fn0mhxef` and `xrv9xvzb`, for T4's wording. Nothing is fitted on them.
