# T1 · Imaging-invariance audit of the frozen model ("lighting augmentation as a test") — P0

Pre-registered part, committed before anything is run (3 Oct 2026). The reading rules are
copied verbatim from `docs/TICKETS.md` §T1 and are not edited after seeing numbers.

## Why

Stage 1 of the frozen model (Batch_3 or not) runs on texture features computed at 2 px
(0.05 µm). The review (§5.1) argues these follow detector noise and sharpness, not material.
Stage 2 (Batch_1 vs Batch_2) runs on InLens DINOv2 components that track the dark-graphite
contrast (§8.2). This experiment measures how far each call moves when only the imaging
changes. **Hypothesis:** the baseline stage moves under noise and blur; the variation stage
moves under the dark-graphite perturbations; the sanity perturbations (gain, contrast,
horizontal flip) do not move the calls.

## Perturbations

Applied to the uint8 channels of a `Field`, then clipped to 0–255. The per-image RNG is
`np.random.default_rng(zlib.crc32(f"{image_id}:{perturbation}".encode()))`.

| id | Channels | Transform | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| gain120 | all | `(img−black)·1.2+black` (reuse `controls._neg_brightness`) | tex_ ≈ unchanged (sanity) |
| contrast080 | all | reuse `controls._neg_contrast(0.8)` | tex_ ≈ unchanged (sanity) |
| black20 | all | `controls._neg_black(20)` | small |
| gamma125 | all | `black + (255−black)·((img−black)/(255−black))^1.25` | ? |
| noise5 | all | `controls._neg_noise(5)`, seeded per image | **key test** |
| blur1 | all | Gaussian σ = 1.0 px (`scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter`) | **key test** |
| curtain | all | `controls._neg_curtaining` | small |
| shade125 | InLens | multiply rows by a linear ramp so top/bottom mean ratio = 1.25 (black-corrected) | ? |
| hflip | all | `img[:, ::-1]` | ≈ invariant (sanity; never vertical flip: vertical is through-thickness) |
| dg_add | InLens | graphite pixels (`segment()` mask == `Phase.GRAPHITE`) in the top 40% of valid rows: `black + 0.30·(v−black)` | tests the §8.2 Q2 confound |
| dg_remove | InLens | graphite pixels with `v−black < 55`: `black + (v−black)/0.30` | tests the §8.2 Q2 confound |

Details pinned before running:

- `black` is each channel's `qc.measure.black_level`; pixels below it are clipped at it.
- shade125's ramp is normalised to mean 1 (endpoints 10/9 and 8/9, ratio 1.25) so it does not
  double as a gain change; it applies over the whole InLens row range.
- The graphite mask for dg_add / dg_remove is `segment()` on the **original** field at full
  resolution, `== Phase.GRAPHITE`, valid rows only (`qc.measure.valid_rows`).
- Dark share, used to choose which images get dg_remove: graphite pixels in valid rows;
  InLens black-subtracted, Gaussian σ = 1; dark if < 55; pixels at 255 excluded;
  share = dark / graphite.
- dg_add runs on the 7 Batch_1 images; dg_remove on the Batch_2 and Batch_3 images with dark
  share > 0.1; noise5 and blur1 first on the 17 Batch_3 images (review §8.5 step 1). The
  remaining seven perturbations run on all 31 known images. Reading "the rest of the table"
  as those seven: noise5/blur1 and the dg_ pair keep their ticket-scoped image sets, which is
  what the reading rules need.
- Features use the same path as `attribute_images`: `features.image_features(field)` +
  `deep.deep_features(field)` merged like `merge_deep`, cached per
  `(image_id, perturbation)` in `out/experiments/T1/perturbed_features.parquet` (originals
  cached under `perturbation="none"` from `out/features.csv`). `out/features.csv` is not
  rewritten.

## Two prediction arms (both reported; arm B is the headline)

- **A. Frozen as is:** `attribute.load_model()` + `attribute.predict()` — the live path,
  in-sample (the model was trained on these 31 images), temperature-scaled as shipped.
- **B. Out of sample:** for each strip (`attribute.strip_group`), refit the frozen recipe on
  the other strips — staged `(("reg","edge","tex","par","kpi"), ("deep",))`, baseline
  `Batch_3`, fixed `C` from the frozen JSON (baseline stage 0.03, variation stage 0.01), no
  nested `C` search — and predict the original and the perturbed images of the held-out
  strip. Arm B probabilities are **uncalibrated** (temperature 1).

## Exact metrics

Per perturbation × true batch, for each arm:

- the number of `predicted` calls that change (against the same arm's call on the original);
- the number of `stage_baseline.call` changes;
- the number of `stage_variation.call` changes;
- the median |Δ `p_Batch_3`|;
- per family (`tex, reg, par, kpi, edge, deep PCs`): the median |Δfeature| / (SD of that
  feature over the Batch_3 images). Deep columns are compared as the 10 PCs of the frozen
  model's variation-stage reducer.

Plus the robustness curve on 3 Batch_3 images from different strips — contrast factors
{0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5} and noise σ {0, 2, 5, 8, 12} — plotting `p_Batch_3` (arm B) and the
drift of `si_graphite_ratio`, `si_d50_um` and `porosity_apparent` (`measure.kpis`) against
strength: `docs/experiments/T1_robustness.png`.

## Reading rules (pre-registered, verbatim from the ticket)

- If gain120 or contrast080 moves tex_ by more than 0.2 SD, the table in review §5.1 is wrong. Say so.
- If noise5 or blur1 flips `stage_baseline` for ≥ 3 of 17 Batch_3 images in arm B, the first stage reads the microscope. Consequence: wording (T4) and the pitch's limits. The model stays frozen.
- If dg_add moves ≥ 4 of 7 Batch_1 images off Batch_1 in `stage_variation` (arm B), the Batch_1/Batch_2 lean rests on the electrical contrast.

## Time box

About 2 h of CPU, in the ticket's order: (1) noise5 + blur1 on the 17 Batch_3 images;
(2) dg_add on the 7 Batch_1 and dg_remove on the dark-share > 0.1 Batch_2/Batch_3 images;
(3) the remaining seven perturbations on all 31; (4) the robustness PNG. If (3) overruns,
(1), (2) and (4) still stand and the gap is reported.

## Results

_(appended after the run; the rules above were not edited)_

Run 3–4 Oct 2026 on the 31 known images. 272 perturbed rows at ~11 s per perturbed image,
about 50 min for features, plus ~7 min for the curve; `out/experiments/T1/run.log` has the
progress. Outputs: `out/experiments/T1/perturbed_features.parquet` (272 perturbed + 31
`none` rows, keyed `(image_id, perturbation)`), `report_by_arm.csv`, `family_drift.csv`,
`dark_shares.csv`, `robustness_curve.csv`, and the PNG below.

Context: on the unperturbed images arm A (in-sample) calls 30/31 correctly; arm B (the
frozen recipe refit per held-out strip, uncalibrated) calls 23/31. Dark-graphite shares
(`out/experiments/T1/dark_shares.csv`, the T2 definition) land inside review §8.2's ranges:
Batch_1 0.01–0.07, Batch_2 0.00–0.18, Batch_3 0.00–0.70; 14 of 31 images are above 0.1 and
got dg_remove (4 Batch_2, 10 Batch_3).

### Robustness curve

`docs/experiments/T1_robustness.png`: p_Batch_3 (arm B) and the drift of
`si_graphite_ratio`, `si_d50_um`, `porosity_apparent` on 3 Batch_3 strips (0grcilhi,
71vgq3fw, 9luzk4jm). p_Batch_3 is flat under contrast 0.5–1.5 (0.73–1.00) but collapses
under noise: at σ = 5 the three images read 0.32 / 0.07 / 0.62, at σ = 8 all are ≈ 0.
`si_graphite_ratio` and `porosity_apparent` barely move; `si_d50_um` inflates under noise
(3.1 → 3.7–4.0 µm at σ = 5–8): the call fails well before the KPIs do.

### The three readings

1. **gain120 moved tex_ by 0.062 SD and contrast080 by 0.088 SD — both under 0.2 SD.** The
   table in review §5.1 is not wrong: brightness and contrast leave the texture features
   essentially unchanged, as it argues.
2. **noise5 flips `stage_baseline` for 12 of 17 Batch_3 images in arm B (all from Batch_3
   to "not Batch_3"), and blur1 for 3 of 17** (the three were arm-B misses that blur
   happens to push back onto Batch_3: pl8uabbv, utfgcjfa, vc2whyaq). The rule's condition
   is met — ≥ 3 of 17 — so: **the first stage reads the microscope.** Consequence per the
   rule: wording (T4) and the pitch's limits; the model stays frozen. The effect is large,
   not marginal: median |Δp_Batch_3| is 0.62 for noise5, p_Batch_3 collapses to ≈ 0 by
   σ = 8 on the curve, and tex_ moves 7.6 SD under blur1.
3. **dg_add moves all 7 of 7 Batch_1 images off Batch_1 in arm B** — and further than the
   rule imagined: every perturbed image is called *Batch_3* (stage_baseline flips too;
   p_Batch_3 goes from 0.02–0.18 to 0.56–0.90). The rule's condition (≥ 4 of 7) is met, so:
   **the Batch_1/Batch_2 lean rests on the electrical contrast.** Two honest notes: the
   pull is towards the baseline, not just inside the variation stage; and it does not run
   only through the deep family — darkening graphite moves `par_inlens_ratio_p50/iqr`,
   the particle-type shares and `tex_inlens_glcm_*` by 1–66 SD as well as the deep PCs by
   0.72 SD. The InLens dark-graphite contrast reaches both stages. dg_remove shows the
   same lever in reverse on a smaller scale (2 of 10 Batch_3 dark-zone images change call
   in arm B; 3 change stage_baseline).

### Deviations from the ticket, noted

- "The rest of the table on all 31" was run as the seven remaining perturbations on all 31;
  noise5/blur1 keep the ticket's Batch_3 scope and the dg_ pair keeps its ticket-scoped
  sets (that is all the reading rules need).
- Arm B fits the frozen recipe through `_fit_parts` (the same stage fit `fit_model` uses)
  with the JSON's fixed per-stage `C`, skipping the calibration/nested-LOSO inside
  `fit_model`; probabilities are temperature 1 as pre-registered. `_fit_parts` learned a
  dict form of `C` for this; default behaviour is unchanged and tested.
- shade125's ramp is mean-1 with endpoints in ratio 1.25 (10/9 → 8/9), so it does not
  double as a gain change; dg_remove's pixel test uses the raw `v − black < 55` as the
  table says (the σ = 1 smoothing is only in the share used to select images).
- The first run was killed (no traceback, probably memory pressure) during step 4; step 4
  was rerun alone from the parquet cache with identical inputs — the reported tables are
  from the first run and the PNG from the rerun (both deterministic).
