# T9 · Remove what the measured imaging explains, then re-evaluate

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T9. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §5.1(c, d), [L41]. Stacked on T2 (it extends T2's `residualize` hook). Diagnostic: it scores candidate representations. The frozen model is not changed (Pat, 3 Oct: frozen until a clearly better model exists). Data: the 31 known images.

## Pre-registered (committed before any run)

**Question.** Does the attribution signal survive once everything the measured imaging descriptors predict linearly is removed from the features?

**Covariates (fixed now).**

- The 21 `img_` descriptors **excluding** `img_bse_p1`, `img_bse_p50` and `img_bse_p99`. BSE brightness percentiles follow composition (more silicon = brighter), so removing them would remove material.
- Plus `dark_graphite_share` from T2.
- **Inside each training fold:** standardise the 22 covariates and reduce them to their first **k = 3** principal components (fit on the training rows), then regress on those. 22 covariates against about 28 training rows would otherwise fit everything.

**Erasure.** Inside each fold (and inside the inner `C` loop's training data), OLS on an intercept plus the k covariate PCs.

- Coefficients are fitted on the training rows and applied to the held-out rows, using their own measured covariates (imaging is measurable on any new image).
- Scope "all": every model column (named features and deep PCs) is residualised.
- This gives zero training-set covariance between every feature and the covariates: the criterion LEACE [L41] guarantees, without LEACE's minimal-edit property. No new dependency.

**Runs.** LOSO, nested `C`; each with a null of 200 segment shuffles, seed 0, through the same pipeline.

| # | Question | Families | Without erasure (reproduce) | With erasure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Batch_3 vs rest | `tex` | expect ≈ 0.88 (PLAN_v4 §12) | run |
| 2 | Batch_3 vs rest | material (`reg, edge, tex, par, kpi`) | expect ≈ 0.85 | run |
| 3 | Three-way | flat `deep` | 0.64 | run |
| 4 | Three-way | staged `material > deep` (frozen recipe) | 0.66 | run |

Sensitivity, not used for the readings: runs 1 and 4 with k = 1 and k = 5.

**Reading rules.**

- If run 1 or run 2 with erasure is at or below its null p95: the Batch_3-vs-rest signal of the named features is explained by the measured imaging. The first stage reads the microscope, and T4 words its reasons accordingly.
- If run 4 with erasure is at or below its null p95: the frozen recipe's whole three-way signal is explained by measured imaging. Say so next to every call.
- If both stay above their null: the signal is not explained by the measured imaging descriptors. This does not exclude unmeasured acquisition effects.
- The model stays frozen. Any erased representation is a v2 candidate only, and counts as one entry in the best-of-list.

### Post-hoc control (NOT pre-registered; added after seeing the results above)

Question: does erasing 3 covariate PCs destroy signal at n = 31 regardless of what the covariates are?

Method: the same 22 covariates with their rows shuffled across images, which keeps their covariance and breaks their link to the images. 20 draws. Command: `PYTHONPATH=.:scripts/experiments uv run python scripts/experiments/T9_control.py` → `out/experiments/T9/control.json`.

| Run | Plain | Erase shuffled covariates: mean (p05–p95) | Erase real imaging covariates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Batch_3 vs rest, `tex` | 0.88 | 0.81 (0.74–0.88) | 0.50 |
| 4 Three-way, staged | 0.66 | 0.60 (0.48–0.70) | 0.46 |

- For texture, the collapse is specific to the imaging: erasing unrelated directions costs about 0.07, erasing imaging costs 0.38.
- For the three-way model, erasure alone costs about 0.06 and the real erasure lands just under the shuffled p05. Mostly imaging-specific, but less clear-cut.

### What this means, and what it does not

- **Every attribution signal we have can be predicted linearly from how the images were acquired** (brightness, noise, sharpness, saturation and curtaining of the three detectors, plus the dark-graphite contrast). Only the composition-sensitive BSE percentiles were left out.
- **It does not prove the folders differ only by microscope.** Imaging sessions follow strips (PLAN_v4 §1.1: strip 2060 is the imaging outlier; T2: the dark share follows whole strips), and the organisers built folders from strips. Session and folder are therefore entangled in this data. A material difference that happens to co-occur with a session is erased too.
- The honest claim is: **with these 31 images, we cannot separate the folder signal from acquisition.** This holds for "Batch_3 or not" as well, which T1–T3 had left standing.
- For the judged calls: if the held-back images were acquired in the same sessions as their folder's strips (T2 §5 found all three unseen samples on known strips), the frozen model will still sort them. The reasons, however, must not be presented as material properties. **This needs Pat's and Patrik's decision on wording for the pitch and T4.**
- Not a v2 candidate: erasing imaging leaves nothing above chance. The way forward is acquisition-robust features (T10 texture at the material scale, T8 augmentation), checked against this erasure.
