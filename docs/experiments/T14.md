# T14 · Confidence methods (report-only)

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T14. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §7.4, [L52]–[L55], [L15]. Report-only: the frozen model's calibration is not changed, and nothing here is a list entry for accuracy. Data: the 31 known images.

## Pre-registered (committed before any run)

**Input.** The out-of-fold (leave-one-strip-out, nested `C`) three-way probabilities of the frozen recipe, staged `material > deep` (`qc.attribute.loso_cv`): 31 rows.

**Today's calibration** (`attribute.calibrate`) fits its temperature and conformal q̂ on the same 31 points it then describes. Here every method is evaluated **leave-one-image-out over those 31 points**: each image's calibrated output uses only the other 30. This is a calibration-level hold-out on top of the strip-held-out probabilities.

| # | Method | Output per image | Compared with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Temperature scaling (today), temperature fitted on the other 30 | three-way probabilities | – |
| 2 | Venn–Abers for stage 1 (Batch_3 or not) [L52]: isotonic regression on the other 30 (score = p_Batch_3, label = is Batch_3), refitted with the image added as label 0 and as label 1 | interval [p0, p1] and the merged p = p1 / (1 − p0 + p1) | stage 1 of method 1: p_Batch_3 |
| 3 | Leave-one-out ("cross") conformal sets [L54] at α = 0.2: q̂ from the other 30 nonconformity scores 1 − p_true (temperature-scaled as in 1); the set is {class: 1 − p ≤ q̂} | prediction set | today's split conformal on all 31 (in-sample) |
| 4 | MAP Bayesian logistic regression [L15]: a Gaussian prior with σ = 2.5 on standardised inputs, i.e. a fixed `C` = σ² = 6.25 in both stages, no inner `C` search. It replaces the `C` grid; the model is otherwise the same, with new out-of-fold probabilities | three-way probabilities | nested `C` |

**Metrics.**

- Stage 1 (methods 1 and 2): Brier score and log loss, and for Venn–Abers the mean interval width.
- Three-way (methods 1 and 4): balanced accuracy, Brier, log loss, and right / n per tier (high ≥ 0.75, medium ≥ 0.5, low).
- Sets (method 3 against today): coverage and mean set size.

**Reading.** Report-only. A method is "worth switching to after the freeze" if it improves the log loss of its comparison **and** does not reduce coverage below 0.8 (for sets). At n = 31, differences under about 0.05 in Brier are within noise [L46]; that is stated with every number.

## Results (4 Oct 2026, after the pre-registration commit `fb36e2d`)

Command: `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/experiments/T14_confidence.py`. Output: `out/experiments/T14/result.json`. Every number is on 31 images; Brier differences under about 0.05 are noise [L46].

**Three-way, the frozen recipe's out-of-fold probabilities:**

| Probabilities | Balanced accuracy | Brier | Log loss | Right per tier: high / medium / low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (no temperature) | 0.66 | 0.39 | **0.79** | 11/11, 4/9, 7/11 |
| Temperature fitted leave-one-out (today's method, held out) | 0.66 | 0.41 | 0.94 | 9/9, 5/9, 8/13 |
| MAP prior σ = 2.5 (`C` = 6.25), raw | 0.64 | 0.53 | 1.55 | 17/23, 4/8, 0/0 |
| MAP prior, temperature leave-one-out | 0.64 | 0.43 | **0.75** | 11/12, 5/10, 5/9 |

**Stage 1 (Batch_3 or not):**

| | Brier | Log loss | Mean interval width |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature (leave-one-out) | 0.125 | 0.64 | – |
| Venn–Abers (leave-one-out) | 0.144 | **0.46** | 0.21 |

**Prediction sets (α = 0.2):**

| | Coverage | Mean size |
|---|---|---|
| Today: split conformal, q̂ fitted on all 31 (in-sample) | 0.87 | 1.48 |
| Leave-one-out conformal | 0.84 | 1.45 |

**Readings, as pre-registered:**

- **Venn–Abers for stage 1: worth switching to after the freeze.** Log loss falls from 0.64 to 0.46; Brier rises by 0.02, which is noise. The 0.21-wide interval is the honest statement of how little 31 points pin the probability down.
- **Leave-one-out conformal: worth switching to.** It keeps coverage above 0.8 (0.84) at the same set size, and it is an honest number where today's 0.87 is in-sample.
- **MAP prior instead of the `C` grid: worth switching to only with a temperature.** On its own it is badly overconfident (log loss 1.55). With a leave-one-out temperature it has the best three-way log loss (0.75) at one image lower accuracy.

**Not pre-registered, but the clearest finding:** today's temperature step, evaluated held out, makes the three-way log loss *worse* than no temperature at all (0.94 against 0.79). The frozen model's temperature (1.42) was fitted and described on the same 31 points, so its tier records (9/9, 5/9, 8/13) are slightly optimistic. The held-out tiers here are 9/9, 5/9, 8/13 too, because the temperature hardly moves between folds. So the **tier records stand**, but the temperature itself adds nothing.

## Adoption (4 Oct 2026, after the results above; not pre-registered)

The model was unfrozen to take these methods in. Before changing `qc/attribute.py`, each variant was scored in two views, because the comparison above holds out one image while the rest of the code holds out a strip, and because it never scored an image the classifier had not seen.

Command: `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/experiments/T14_adoption.py`. Output: `out/experiments/T14/adoption.json`.

- **A. The 31 known images.** Strip-held-out probabilities, calibrated with the points of the other strips only.
- **B. The rehearsal.** 30 draws of 9 unseen images (3 per batch); classifier and calibration refitted on the other 22. 270 calls.

"Staged call" means: the baseline if it is at least as likely as not, else the leading variation. "Argmax" is the frozen rule: the largest of the three probabilities.

| Variant | A: right, balanced | A: tiers high / medium / low | A: log loss, stage 1 | B: right, balanced | B: tiers high / medium / low | B: log loss, stage 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Temperature, argmax (frozen 3 Oct) | 22/31, 0.66 | 9/9, 6/10, 7/12 | 0.95, 0.64 | 153/270, 0.57 | 58/63, 30/51, 65/156 | 0.79, 0.33 |
| 1 No calibration, argmax | 22/31, 0.66 | 11/11, 4/9, 7/11 | 0.79, 0.49 | 153/270, 0.57 | 82/87, 9/46, 62/137 | 0.77, 0.31 |
| 2 No calibration, staged call | 22/31, 0.68 | 11/11, 4/9, 7/11 | 0.79, 0.49 | 176/270, 0.65 | 82/87, 9/46, 85/137 | 0.77, 0.31 |
| 3 Venn–Abers as above, argmax | 20/31, 0.56 | 7/7, 9/13, 4/11 | 0.74, 0.44 | 135/270, 0.50 | 71/75, 15/61, 49/134 | 0.92, 0.46 |

| **5 Class-balanced Venn–Abers, staged call (adopted)** | 22/31, 0.66 | 9/9, 7/11, 6/11 | **0.72, 0.42** | **178/270, 0.66** | 56/58, 25/52, 97/160 | 0.84, 0.38 |

**What this adds to the readings above:**

- **The temperature goes.** Without it the log loss is lower in both views. This confirms the reading above.
- **Venn–Abers as recorded cannot go in as is.** It holds up on the known images (stage-1 log loss 0.44 against 0.49), but it is worse than no calibration on unseen draws (0.46 against 0.31). The isotonic fit carries the training mix into every call: 55% to 64% of the calibration points are Batch_3, the rehearsal draws are one third Batch_3, and an image with a middling score is then called Batch_3 at 50% to 75% and is wrong three times out of four (15/61).
- **Weighting the two labels equally repairs most of it.** This is the convention the classifier already uses (`class_weight="balanced"`), and the mix of the unseen images is not known. Variant 5 is the best on the known images and the most accurate in the rehearsal.
- **Calibrated probabilities need the staged call.** With argmax, Batch_3 wins whenever the two variations split the rest evenly, and accuracy falls (variant 3). The staged call follows the model's own first stage. It is worth about 23 of 270 rehearsal calls on its own (variant 2 against 1).
- **The prediction sets** now report a held-out coverage. Known images: 0.87 at 1.5 batches. Rehearsal (`--dry-run --repeats 30`): 0.95 at 2.1 batches, against 0.91 at 1.9 for the frozen model.

**What it costs, and the limits:**

- On unseen draws the log loss is higher than with no calibration (0.84 against 0.77). With 22 to 31 calibration points Venn–Abers cannot say more than about 90%, so clear calls are stated less sharply. We accept that: the high tier is right 56 of 58 times, and a ceiling near 90% is the honest statement at this sample size.
- In the rehearsal the medium tier (25/52) is still below the low tier (97/160). Medium is mostly weak Batch_3 calls, low is mostly Batch_1 against Batch_2. The tiers separate cleanly only at the top.
- The variant was chosen on the same 31 images it is scored on, among six. Differences of one or two images in view A are noise. The rehearsal gain (0.57 to 0.66) is the result that carries the decision, and most of it comes from the call rule.
- MAP prior instead of the `C` grid: not adopted. It needs a temperature, and the classifier would no longer be the one evaluated everywhere else.

**In the model since 4 Oct** (`config/attribution_model.json`, version v4.2): the same two logistic regressions as the frozen model (identical coefficients), class-balanced Venn–Abers for "Batch_3 or not" with its interval, the staged call, no temperature, and held-out records for tiers, stages and sets. The three test samples keep their bets (`results/Hackathon-Polaron-test.refit.json`).
