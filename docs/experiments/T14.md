# T14 · Confidence methods (report-only)

Ticket: [../TICKETS.md](../TICKETS.md) T14. Source: MODEL_LITERATURE_REVIEW §7.4, [L52]–[L55], [L15]. Report-only: the frozen model's calibration is not changed, and nothing here is a list entry for accuracy. Data: the 31 known images.

## Pre-registered (committed before any run)

**Input.** The out-of-fold (leave-one-strip-out, nested `C`) three-way probabilities of the frozen recipe, staged `material > deep` (`qc.attribute.loso_cv`): 31 rows.

**Today's calibration** (`attribute.calibrate`) fits its temperature and conformal q̂ on the same 31 points it then describes. Here every method is evaluated **leave-one-image-out over those 31 points**: each image's calibrated output uses only the other 30. This is a calibration-level hold-out on top of the strip-held-out probabilities.

| # | Method | Output per image | Compared with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Temperature scaling (today), temperature fitted on the other 30 | three-way probabilities | – |
| 2 | Venn–Abers for stage 1 (Batch_3 or not) [L52]: isotonic regression on the other 30 (score = p_Batch_3, label = is Batch_3), refitted with the image added as label 0 and as label 1 | interval [p0, p1] and the merged p = p1 / (1 − p0 + p1) | stage 1 of method 1: p_Batch_3 |
| 3 | Leave-one-out ("cross") conformal sets [L54] at α = 0.2: q̂ from the other 30 nonconformity scores 1 − p_true (temperature-scaled as in 1); the set is {class: 1 − p ≤ q̂} | prediction set | today's split conformal on all 31 (in-sample) |
| 4 | MAP Bayesian logistic regression [L15]: a Gaussian prior with σ = 2.5 on standardised inputs, i.e. a fixed `C` = σ² = 6.25 in both stages, no inner `C` search. It replaces the `C` grid; the model is otherwise the same, with new out-of-fold probabilities | three-way probabilities | nested `C` |

**Metrics.**

- Stage 1 (methods 1 and 2): Brier score and log loss, and for Venn–Abers the mean interval width.
- Three-way (methods 1 and 4): balanced accuracy, Brier, log loss, and right / n per tier (high ≥ 0.75, medium ≥ 0.5, low).
- Sets (method 3 against today): coverage and mean set size.

**Reading.** Report-only. A method is "worth switching to after the freeze" if it improves the log loss of its comparison **and** does not reduce coverage below 0.8 (for sets). At n = 31, differences under about 0.05 in Brier are within noise [L46]; that is stated with every number.

## Results (4 Oct 2026, after the pre-registration commit `fb36e2d`)

Command: `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/experiments/T14_confidence.py`. Output: `out/experiments/T14/result.json`. Every number is on 31 images; Brier differences under about 0.05 are noise [L46].

**Three-way, the frozen recipe's out-of-fold probabilities:**

| Probabilities | Balanced accuracy | Brier | Log loss | Right per tier: high / medium / low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (no temperature) | 0.66 | 0.39 | **0.79** | 11/11, 4/9, 7/11 |
| Temperature fitted leave-one-out (today's method, held out) | 0.66 | 0.41 | 0.94 | 9/9, 5/9, 8/13 |
| MAP prior σ = 2.5 (`C` = 6.25), raw | 0.64 | 0.53 | 1.55 | 17/23, 4/8, 0/0 |

**Prediction sets (α = 0.2):**

| | Coverage | Mean size |
|---|---|---|

**Readings, as pre-registered:**

- **Venn–Abers for stage 1: worth switching to after the freeze.** Log loss falls from 0.64 to 0.46; Brier rises by 0.02, which is noise. The 0.21-wide interval is the honest statement of how little 31 points pin the probability down.
- **Leave-one-out conformal: worth switching to.** It keeps coverage above 0.8 (0.84) at the same set size, and it is an honest number where today's 0.87 is in-sample.
- **MAP prior instead of the `C` grid: worth switching to only with a temperature.** On its own it is badly overconfident (log loss 1.55). With a leave-one-out temperature it has the best three-way log loss (0.75) at one image lower accuracy.

**Not pre-registered, but the clearest finding:** today's temperature step, evaluated held out, makes the three-way log loss *worse* than no temperature at all (0.94 against 0.79). The frozen model's temperature (1.42) was fitted and described on the same 31 points, so its tier records (9/9, 5/9, 8/13) are slightly optimistic. The held-out tiers here are 9/9, 5/9, 8/13 too, because the temperature hardly moves between folds. So the **tier records stand**, but the temperature itself adds nothing.
